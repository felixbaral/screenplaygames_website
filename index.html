„Together we combine the interactive art of games with the visual storytelling of film.“

We are a team of a game designer with a background in software development and an actress and filmmaker. Combining our expertise, we founded Screen Play Studio. FMV games are at the heart of our Berlin-based studio. All our games will be narrative driven. In addition, we plan to produce films and music videos. Our first project will be the FMV game Burning Bridges.

The representation of minorities especially the queer community is an important part of our company's identity.
